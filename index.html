Our Galaxy

The Milky Way is our home galaxy — a vast spiral of hundreds of billions of stars. The glowing band and starfield you see are a view from inside it; our entire solar system is just one tiny neighborhood within this galaxy.

Constellations

Constellations are patterns people have traced between bright stars to tell stories and find their way. Turn on Constellations to see famous ones; hover a constellation name for its story.

The Solar System

At the center is the Sun. Around it orbit eight planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Click any body or use the strip to fly there.

Moons, Dwarfs & Comets

Moons circle planets; dwarf planets (Ceres, Eris, Makemake, Haumea) and Halley’s comet follow their own paths.
